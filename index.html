GhostBell Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GhostBell ("GhostBell", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the GhostBell mobile application.

GhostBell is an application designed to help teachers and lesson providers organize lesson schedules and use configurable audio bell alerts.

1. Developer

App: GhostBell

2. No User Account Required

GhostBell does not require you to create a user account, username, or password.

The application uses an anonymous installation identifier to associate certain Premium subscription information with an installation of the application.

This identifier is not intended to directly identify you as a person.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

GhostBell stores certain application data locally on your device so that the application can provide its scheduling and bell functionality.

This may include:

  • Weekly lesson schedules.
  • Today's schedule changes.
  • Working-day preferences.
  • Lesson names and related lesson information entered by you.
  • Bell and reminder settings.
  • Selected bell sounds.
  • Custom sound files imported by you.
  • Application language and theme preferences.
  • Locally generated application identifiers.

This information is primarily stored locally on your device and is not automatically uploaded to our servers merely because you create or edit a schedule.

4. Anonymous Installation Identifier

GhostBell generates an anonymous UUID for each application installation.

This identifier is used to:

  • Identify an installation when communicating with the GhostBell backend.
  • Associate Google Play Premium entitlement information with an installation.
  • Support Premium entitlement restoration.

The identifier is not intended to identify your real-world identity.

5. Premium Subscription Information

GhostBell offers optional Premium functionality through Google Play.

When you purchase or restore Premium, the GhostBell backend may process information required to verify and maintain your subscription entitlement, including:

  • Anonymous installation identifier.
  • Google Play product ID.
  • Google Play purchase token.
  • Subscription state.
  • Purchase time.
  • Subscription expiration time.
  • Acknowledgement status.
  • Revocation or refund state where applicable.
  • Technical timestamps required to maintain subscription records.

This information is used to:

  • Verify Google Play purchases.
  • Activate Premium functionality.
  • Restore Premium access.
  • Detect expiration, cancellation, revocation, or refund.
  • Prevent unauthorized or fraudulent Premium access.
  • Maintain subscription records.

GhostBell does not receive or store your credit-card number, debit-card number, bank account details, or other payment credentials used by Google Play.

6. Google Play Services

Premium purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.

GhostBell communicates with Google Play services and its backend verification system to verify purchases and maintain Premium entitlement status.

Google may independently collect and process information according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

7. Information We Do Not Collect

GhostBell does not intentionally collect the following information for user-account or advertising purposes:

  • Your name for account creation.
  • Your password.
  • Your contacts.
  • Your SMS messages.
  • Your phone call information.
  • Your precise location.
  • Your payment card details.

GhostBell does not require user accounts and does not display third-party advertising.

8. How We Use Information

Information handled by GhostBell is used to operate and maintain the application's functionality.

We may use information to:

  1. Provide GhostBell's scheduling and bell functionality.
  2. Store and restore Premium subscription entitlement.
  3. Verify Google Play purchases.
  4. Detect subscription expiration, cancellation, refunds, or revocations.
  5. Prevent abuse or unauthorized Premium access.
  6. Maintain and troubleshoot the service.
  7. Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

9. Information Sharing

GhostBell does not sell user information.

Information may be processed by service providers necessary to operate the application, including Google Play and services used for subscription verification and GhostBell backend infrastructure.

Such processing is limited to what is reasonably necessary to provide, secure, and maintain the relevant service.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by GhostBell.

These measures may include:

  • Secure local storage mechanisms for the anonymous installation identifier.
  • HTTPS communication between the application and backend services.
  • Server-side verification of Google Play purchase tokens.
  • Restricted handling of Google service credentials on the server.
  • Access controls for backend and database infrastructure.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

Local application data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the application, or otherwise remove it through available device or application controls.

Anonymous Premium entitlement records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Premium services, restore and verify purchases, detect fraud or abuse, maintain transaction integrity, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, reasonable steps will be taken to delete or anonymize it, subject to legitimate retention requirements.

12. Data Deletion

GhostBell does not create traditional user accounts.

Because there is no GhostBell username or account profile, there is no traditional user account that needs to be deleted.

You may remove locally stored GhostBell information by using the application's available reset functionality or by uninstalling the application, subject to the normal behavior of your device and operating system.

If you would like us to review or delete an anonymous Premium entitlement record associated with your installation, you may contact us using the privacy contact address below.

For legitimate transaction, fraud-prevention, security, or legal reasons, certain records may need to be retained for an appropriate period.

13. Children's Privacy

GhostBell is a general productivity and scheduling application intended for teachers and lesson providers.

GhostBell is not specifically directed toward children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of creating user accounts or profiles.

14. Third-Party Services

GhostBell may rely on third-party services necessary for application distribution, subscription processing, backend infrastructure, and technical operation.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in GhostBell's functionality, services, legal requirements, or data practices.

When material changes are made, the updated policy will be published at the same public Privacy Policy URL and the "Last Updated" date will be changed.
